# Welcome to the Humanoid Robotics Book!

This is the official Docusaurus home page for our exploration into the fascinating world of humanoid robotics. Here, you'll find comprehensive guides, tutorials, and insights into the latest advancements and foundational concepts in humanoid robotics.

## What is Humanoid Robotics?

Humanoid robots are advanced machines designed to resemble the human body in form and function. They are at the forefront of artificial intelligence, mechanical engineering, and computer science, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in automation and human-robot interaction.
